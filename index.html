How it works

Plan. Shop. Cook.

Weeknights are busy. SeasonMe takes the thinking out of dinner so you can get on with the eating.

1

Plan your week

Pick recipes for the week and give each one a day. Everything scales to your household.

2

Shop once

Your grocery list builds itself – ingredients added up and sorted by aisle, ready to tick off.

3

Cook with confidence

Step-by-step cook mode shows exactly what each step needs, with timers and the screen kept on.

Your recipes, from anywhere

Saved a recipe on social? Bring it in.

Paste a link from a recipe website, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Facebook. SeasonMe pulls in the title, photo, ingredients and method – even from what’s said in a TikTok video.

Made for the way you eat

Your menu, your rules.

Tell SeasonMe how you eat and it only suggests recipes that fit.

  • Nine menu types, including vegetarian, vegan, keto, low carb and pescatarian
  • Hide recipes with ingredients you’d rather avoid
  • Nutrition per serving, with filters for calories, carbs, protein, fat and sodium
  • Metric or imperial – oven temperatures included
Eat with the seasons, waste less

Cook what’s fresh – and what you already have.

Seasonal produce tastes better, costs less and travels less. SeasonMe shows what’s in season where you live, with tips on choosing and storing it, and recipes that use it.

  • Northern or southern hemisphere – the right season for where you are
  • “Use what you have” finds recipes for the food in your fridge, starting with what needs using soon
  • Fun food facts and kitchen tips along the way
