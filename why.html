Why SeasonMe

Waste less. Eat seasonal.

SeasonMe helps you waste less and eat better. Cook with what’s already in your fridge, choose what’s in season, and support local growers. It’s better flavour for you and a lighter footprint for the planet.

Our mission

To help every household eat better with less waste, by cooking with what’s already in the kitchen and what’s in season right now.

Our vision

A world where sustainable living starts at home: where leftovers become tomorrow’s dinner, local growers thrive, and every plate follows the seasons.

What we believe

Four simple ideas behind every meal plan.

Use what you have.

Too much good food ends up in the bin. SeasonMe starts with what’s already in your kitchen, beginning with the food that needs using soon, so less goes to waste and more of your grocery money goes into meals.

In season tastes better.

Produce picked at its peak has more flavour and is often more nutritious than food harvested early to survive a long journey.

Local is lighter on the planet.

Eating with the seasons means less long-distance transport, less time in cold storage and lower greenhouse gas emissions. It also supports the growers in your community.

Variety, the natural way.

Changing what you eat through the year brings natural variety to your plate, and a varied diet is good for your gut. Think hydrating fruit and vegetables in summer, and root vegetables and citrus in winter.

Small changes in your kitchen add up to a big difference.
