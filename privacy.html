Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

SeasonMe is a meal-planning app for iPhone, made by SeasonMe Limited in New Zealand (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use SeasonMe. The short version: SeasonMe has no accounts, and everything you add stays on your phone. We don’t collect, sell or share your personal information, and there is no advertising or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your recipes, photos you add, meal plans, grocery lists, kitchen items, reminders and settings (such as allergies, menu type, household size and your country) are stored only on your iPhone. We can’t see them. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Reset app, deletes them permanently.

Importing recipes from a link

When you paste a link to import a recipe, SeasonMe downloads that page directly from your phone, the same way a web browser would, and reads the recipe from it. The website (for example a recipe blog, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Facebook) receives a normal request from your phone and its own privacy policy applies. For TikTok videos, SeasonMe may also download TikTok’s automatic captions for that video. If a recipe page links elsewhere, SeasonMe may follow that link too. Nothing about your imports is sent to us.

If you tap “Find the full recipe” or “Open the original recipe”, a web page opens in your browser; the search provider or website you visit sees that visit.

Location (optional)

If you choose “Use my current location”, SeasonMe asks iOS for your approximate location once, to work out your country and season. iOS may use Apple’s services to turn the location into a place name. SeasonMe saves only the country and city on your phone, never your exact position, and doesn’t send it to us. You can choose your country from a list instead.

Photos and notifications

If you add a photo to a recipe, SeasonMe asks for access to your photo library and stores a copy of the photo you choose on your phone only. Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone; they aren’t sent through our servers.

Contacting us

When you send feedback or contact us from the app, SeasonMe opens your own email app with the message ready for you to send to info@seasonmeapp.com. The email includes the SeasonMe version and, on Contact us, your iPhone model and iOS version unless you untick “Attach app version and device model”, to help us fix problems. We use your email only to reply to you and to improve SeasonMe, and we keep it only as long as needed for that.

Children

SeasonMe doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don’t hold your app data, you control it directly on your phone. If you’ve emailed us, you can ask to see, correct or delete that correspondence by writing to info@seasonmeapp.com. If you’re in New Zealand, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Changes to this policy

If SeasonMe starts collecting information – for example if we add accounts or household sharing in a future version – we’ll update this policy and the app’s App Store privacy details before that version is released.
