Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · applies from SeasonMe 1.1

SeasonMe is a meal-planning app for iPhone, made by SeasonMe Limited in New Zealand (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information SeasonMe collects, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. We don’t sell your personal information, there is no advertising in SeasonMe, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Your SeasonMe account

From version 1.1 you sign in to SeasonMe so your recipes and plans are backed up and appear on every phone you use. You can sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email address. We receive:

  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide it, and we receive a private relay address from Apple instead);
  • your country and menu type, which SeasonMe needs to show seasonal food and suitable recipes;
  • if you choose to give them, your age range, gender and whether you’d like news and offers by email. These are optional and you can change them any time in Settings › Account.

What’s backed up

SeasonMe backs up what you add so you can sign in on another phone and pick up where you left off: your recipes and the photos you add to them, meal plans, grocery lists, kitchen items and settings (such as restrictions, menu type, household size, units and reminders). Photos are shrunk on your phone before they’re uploaded. Your backup is private to your account; nobody else can see it.

Sharing a recipe

When you share a recipe as a link, a copy of that recipe and its photo is saved so the person you send it to can open it in SeasonMe. Anyone who has the link can see that recipe, but shared recipes can’t be browsed or searched. Your name and email aren’t included.

AI features (SeasonMe Plus)

Smart Import, cookbook import and Ask AI use artificial intelligence provided by Anthropic (the maker of Claude). When you use them, SeasonMe sends Anthropic only what’s needed for that request:

  • Smart Import: the link you paste, the text of that web page, the post’s caption and any captions of what’s said in the video, and any text you paste;
  • Cookbook import: the photos of the page you take or choose (shrunk first);
  • Ask AI: your choices (meal, time, style and foods to use), your menu type, restrictions, household size, country and season, and the names of recipes you already have so the ideas are new.

Your name and email address aren’t sent. Anthropic processes this to return the result and, under its commercial terms, doesn’t use it to train its models. See Anthropic’s privacy policy. We keep a count of how many AI requests you make each month so we can apply your plan’s allowance; we don’t keep the content of the requests.

Photos for Ask AI recipes are made by fal.ai from a short description of the dish (for example “Lemon chicken and asparagus”). Nothing about you is sent to fal.ai. The photos are stored by SeasonMe so your saved recipes keep them.

Importing recipes from a link

For the standard (non-AI) import, SeasonMe downloads the page directly from your phone, the same way a web browser would, and reads the recipe from it. The website (for example a recipe blog, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Facebook) receives a normal request from your phone and its own privacy policy applies. SeasonMe may follow a link from a post to the recipe page it mentions.

YouTube. To read a YouTube video’s title, description and photo, SeasonMe uses YouTube API Services: the video link is sent to YouTube (Google). By importing from YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles that request under the Google Privacy Policy. SeasonMe doesn’t access your YouTube or Google account. On your phone, SeasonMe may also read the captions YouTube or TikTok shares for a video.

Subscriptions

SeasonMe Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment and we never see your card or bank details. We receive from Apple whether your subscription or free trial is active and when it renews, so SeasonMe can unlock Plus features.

Location (optional)

If you choose “Use my current location”, SeasonMe asks iOS for your approximate location once, to work out your country and season. iOS may use Apple’s services to turn the location into a place name. SeasonMe saves only the country and city, never your exact position. You can choose your country from a list instead.

Camera, photos and notifications

SeasonMe asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo or photograph a cookbook page. Reminders are notifications scheduled on your phone.

Feedback and Contact us

When you send feedback or a message from the app, we receive what you wrote, your email address if you give one, the SeasonMe version and, on Contact us, your iPhone model and iOS version unless you untick that option. If you aren’t signed in, the message is delivered through Formspree, a form service (see Formspree’s privacy policy). We use messages only to reply to you and to improve SeasonMe.

Who helps us

We use these service providers, who handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase – our database, sign-in and file storage (privacy policy);
  • Anthropic – AI for Smart Import, cookbook import and Ask AI;
  • fal.ai – AI food photos for Ask AI recipes;
  • Google (YouTube API Services) – YouTube video details;
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple, payments and subscriptions;
  • Google Workspace – our email, including sign-in codes;
  • Formspree – messages sent while not signed in.

These providers may store or process information outside New Zealand, for example in Australia or the United States. We choose providers that protect information in a way comparable to New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and backup for as long as you have a SeasonMe account. Shared recipe links stay available until you delete your account. Messages are deleted once they’re no longer needed.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time in Settings › Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, backup, photos, shared recipe links and AI photos. Copies in our providers’ routine backups are removed within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a SeasonMe Plus subscription; cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Children

SeasonMe is for people aged 13 and over, and isn’t directed at children.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your information in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by writing to info@seasonmeapp.com. If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your information, you can contact New Zealand’s Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Changes to this policy

If we change how SeasonMe uses your information, we’ll update this policy and the app’s App Store privacy details, and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
