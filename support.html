SeasonMe support

Plan a week of seasonal meals in minutes. SeasonMe builds your grocery list and walks you through every recipe.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email info@seasonmeapp.com, or use Settings › Contact us in the app. We aim to reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I add recipes?

Go to Recipes › Add your own recipe. Paste a link from a recipe website, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Facebook, or type a recipe in. You can also paste recipe text, such as a caption, and SeasonMe sorts it into ingredients and method. With SeasonMe Plus, Smart Import reads videos and captions with AI, and Cookbook reads a recipe from photos of a cookbook or magazine page.

Why didn’t a social media post import fully?

Many creators keep the full recipe on their website rather than in the post. SeasonMe imports whatever the post shares and, if it says the recipe is elsewhere, offers “Find the full recipe” so you can import it from the creator’s site.

How do I change my restrictions, menu type or household size?

Open Settings. Recipes with ingredients on your Restrictions list are hidden, and every recipe and grocery list is scaled to your household. SeasonMe matches ingredient names only, so always check recipes and labels yourself if you have a food allergy.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. Once you sign in (with Apple or a code sent to your email), your recipes, photos, meal plans and grocery lists are backed up to your private SeasonMe account. Sign in with the same account on another iPhone and they come with you.

What is SeasonMe Plus?

Plus adds AI features – Ask AI recipe ideas, Snap a photo of your fridge, Smart Import and cookbook import, and smarter swaps – plus starting a household. It costs US$1.99 a month or US$19.99 a year (or the equivalent in your local currency), with a 7-day free trial. Each AI feature has a monthly allowance, shown on the SeasonMe Plus screen.

How do I cancel SeasonMe Plus?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: open your iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or Settings › SeasonMe Plus › Manage subscription in the app. Plus keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for. If you reinstall SeasonMe or use a new phone, tap Restore purchases.

How does household sharing work?

Someone with SeasonMe Plus starts a household in Settings › Household and shares the invite code. Up to 5 others join free with the code, and everyone shares one meal plan and grocery list. Changes appear on everyone’s phone within about 20 seconds. Your other recipes and settings stay private.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings › Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and backup. It doesn’t cancel a Plus subscription – cancel that in your iPhone’s settings first.

How is nutrition worked out?

Where a recipe website publishes nutrition facts, SeasonMe uses them. Otherwise it estimates them from the ingredients, so treat them as a guide rather than medical advice.
