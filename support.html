SeasonMe support

Plan a week of seasonal meals in minutes. SeasonMe builds your grocery list and walks you through every recipe.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email info@seasonmeapp.com, or use Settings › Contact us in the app. We aim to reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I add recipes?

Go to Recipes › Add your own recipe. Paste a link from a recipe website, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest or Facebook, or type a recipe in. You can also paste recipe text, such as a caption, and SeasonMe sorts it into ingredients and method.

Why didn’t a social media post import fully?

Many creators keep the full recipe on their website rather than in the post. SeasonMe imports whatever the post shares and, if it says the recipe is elsewhere, offers “Find the full recipe” so you can import it from the creator’s site.

How do I change my restrictions, menu type or household size?

Open Settings. Recipes with ingredients on your Restrictions list are hidden, and every recipe and grocery list is scaled to your household. SeasonMe matches ingredient names only, so always check recipes and labels yourself if you have a food allergy.

Is my data backed up?

SeasonMe keeps everything on your iPhone, with no account. If you delete the app or use Settings › Reset app, your recipes and plans are deleted.

How is nutrition worked out?

Where a recipe website publishes nutrition facts, SeasonMe uses them. Otherwise it estimates them from the ingredients, so treat them as a guide rather than medical advice.
